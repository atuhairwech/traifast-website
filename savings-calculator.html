How the calculator works

The calculator assumes that your starting amount remains invested and that you make the same contribution every month. The estimated return is compounded monthly.

Monthly compounding
The monthly return is calculated from the annual return, then applied to the growing balance each month.

Final Balance = Starting Amount + Monthly Contributions + Estimated Compound Returns

This calculator provides an estimate for planning purposes only. Actual savings or investment returns may differ because of fees, taxes, changing interest rates, market performance, contribution timing and the specific terms of the financial product.
