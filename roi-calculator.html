How the calculator works

Enter your original investment, any additional costs and the final amount received. Traifast calculates the total amount invested, your gain or loss and the percentage return on investment.

ROI = (Profit ÷ Total Investment) × 100

Total Investment = Initial Investment + Additional Costs
Profit = Final Amount − Total Investment

A positive ROI means the investment generated a gain. A negative ROI means the investment resulted in a loss.
