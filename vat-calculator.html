How the calculator works

Enter an amount and the VAT rate, then tell Traifast whether the amount already includes VAT. The calculator will show the VAT amount, the price before VAT and the final VAT-inclusive price.

VAT-exclusive amount VAT = Price × VAT Rate ÷ 100

VAT-inclusive amount VAT = VAT-inclusive Price × VAT Rate ÷ (100 + VAT Rate)
