Privacy Policy

How Traifast handles information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

When you contact Traifast or request a quotation, you may provide your name, company or organization, email address, phone number and details of your requirements. These details may include product specifications, quantities, delivery location, preferred delivery date and any additional information you choose to share.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to understand your requirements, respond to enquiries, prepare quotations and communicate with you about related procurement, supply and delivery requests.

Service providers

Information submitted through our website forms may be processed by third-party service providers that support our website and communications. We use these services as necessary to receive, process and respond to your enquiry and support related business communication.

Information processed when you visit

When you visit our website, certain information may be processed automatically, such as your IP address, browser or device information and pages visited. This helps support website functionality, security, reliability and performance, and understand how the website is used.

Information to avoid sending

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information or other highly sensitive personal information through ordinary website forms. Only include information relevant to your enquiry or quotation request.

Questions and requests about your information

You can contact Traifast to ask about information you have submitted, request a correction or ask for its deletion. We will review reasonable requests and respond as appropriate, taking into account the related enquiry and any information that needs to be retained for business or legal purposes. We may ask for information to help confirm that a request relates to you.

Contact Traifast

For privacy questions or requests about your information, email info@traifast.com or call +256 393 104 343.
