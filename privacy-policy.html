Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Traifast Enterprises Company Limited ("Traifast", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information relating to visitors of traifast.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you use our website, online tools, calculators, and other services available through traifast.com.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may receive or process different types of information.

Information you provide directly

If you contact Traifast through an email address, contact form, or another communication method provided on our website, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, telephone number, and the contents of your message.

Information collected automatically

Like many websites, traifast.com may receive technical information when you visit the website. This may include information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, referring pages, approximate location information, and information about how you interact with the website.

Some of this information may be collected automatically by third-party services used on the website.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating traifast.com.
  • Responding to enquiries and communications.
  • Improving our website, content, tools, and user experience.
  • Understanding how visitors use our website.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing abuse.
  • Measuring website and advertising performance.
  • Displaying relevant advertising where applicable.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Website Tools and Calculators

Traifast provides various online tools and calculators, including financial, business, savings, investment, pricing, and other practical calculators.

Information entered into a calculator is generally used to perform the calculation requested by the visitor. Unless a particular tool explicitly provides a submission or storage function, visitors should not assume that information entered into a calculator is stored by Traifast.

Calculator results are provided for informational and educational purposes and should not be treated as professional financial, investment, tax, legal, or other professional advice.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Traifast and third-party service providers may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies.

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on a user's device that can help websites remember information, understand usage, measure performance, and provide relevant services or advertising.

Google states that AdSense may use cookies when ads are displayed on publisher websites. Google also requires publishers to maintain a privacy policy informing visitors about the use of cookies.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some websites or services.

Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
