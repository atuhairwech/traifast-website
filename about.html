ABOUT TRAIFAST

Building a dependable supply partner for growing businesses.

Traifast Enterprises Company Limited is a Ugandan company focused on procurement, supply and distribution. We connect customers with the products they need to operate, build and grow.

Our Purpose

To make it easier for businesses, institutions and communities to access the products, supplies and solutions they need to operate, build and grow.

Our Vision

To become a leading African supply, distribution and value-addition company, connecting businesses, markets and products across Africa and beyond.

Our Mission

To provide reliable procurement, supply and distribution solutions through efficient sourcing, strong supplier relationships, technology and responsible operations.

Our Values

  • Integrity
  • Reliability
  • Quality
  • Customer focus
  • Efficiency
  • Innovation
