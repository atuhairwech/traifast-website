How the Traifast Profit Calculator works

Enter the amount you pay for each item, your intended selling price and the number of items involved. Then add the costs associated with bringing those products to the customer.

Total Actual Cost Product Investment + Transport + Packaging + Labour + Other Expenses
Estimated Net Profit Total Sales Revenue − Total Actual Cost
Markup Profit per Item ÷ Buying Price × 100
Net Profit Margin Net Profit ÷ Sales Revenue × 100
ROI Net Profit ÷ Total Actual Cost × 100

The break-even price is the minimum average selling price required to recover both the product cost and the additional expenses entered above.

This calculator is designed for retailers, wholesalers, entrepreneurs, students and anyone who wants to make better pricing and business decisions.
