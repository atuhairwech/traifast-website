Stay Safe from Job Scams

Always verify an opportunity before providing personal information or making any payment.

  • Verify the employer or organization.
  • Check the original source of the vacancy.
  • Use the employer's official website whenever possible.
  • Be cautious of anyone asking for money in exchange for a job.
  • Never share passwords, PINs, or sensitive financial information with unknown people.

Traifast does not guarantee employment and is not responsible for recruitment decisions made by employers.
