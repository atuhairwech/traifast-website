How the Selling Price Calculator works

Enter the amount you spend to acquire one item, any additional cost associated with that item, and the profit margin you want to achieve. Traifast calculates the selling price required to achieve that target.

Actual Cost
Buying Cost + Other Cost
Selling Price
Actual Cost ÷ (1 − Desired Profit Margin)
Profit
Selling Price − Actual Cost

For example, if your actual cost is UGX 22,000 and you want a 30% profit margin, the calculator determines the selling price that gives you that margin.

This tool is useful for retailers, wholesalers, traders, entrepreneurs, students and anyone who wants to price products more intelligently.
